Capturing The Untamed Spirit

Welcome to Ninety Six Wild Lens. We are passionate wildlife photographers dedicated to freezing the raw, unfiltered moments of nature. Our journey takes us through deep forests, vast savannas, and rugged terrains to bring you stories from the heart of the wild.

Every photograph we take is a testament to the beauty of planet Earth and a reminder of the urgent need to conserve its precious inhabitants. Through our lens, we share not just images, but the deep emotions and spirits of the wilderness.

Welcome to our community, where passion for the wild meets the art of photography! We specialize in teaching you how to capture the breathtaking beauty of the natural world through your lens, while sharing expert, field-tested tips on how to spot elusive wild animals in their natural habitats. Whether you are a beginner looking to master your camera settings or an adventurer eager to track wildlife safely and ethically, our mission is to equip you with the skills, patience, and knowledge needed to turn every outdoor journey into an unforgettable visual story.
